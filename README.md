## 🎮 About the Game

Sludgineers is a casual simulation and active incremental game released on August 25, 2026. Developed by Snickerdoodle Games and Funk Games, it follows pollution-cleaning specialists who collect, mine, refine, and sell contaminated materials while restoring the countryside.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Sludgineers**. This trainer enhances Sludgineers by providing optional control over cash, resources, tool performance, movement convenience, upgrade progression, and refinery automation. It is designed for the single-player campaign and helps players focus on exploration, quests, pollution cleanup, and equipment development.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to accelerate pollution cleanup, refinery development, equipment upgrades, area unlocking, and quest progression while preserving the game's relaxing single-player experience.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Cash** — Prevents cash from decreasing when purchasing equipment, upgrades, and other available improvements.
- **Unlimited Resources** — Keeps collected materials available for processing, selling, and refinery progression.

- **Maximum Tool Efficiency** — Improves the effectiveness of cleanup and mining tools, including the vacuum and jackhammer.
- **Unlimited Power-Ups** — Allows power-ups such as the Magnet to remain available for repeated use.

- **Automation Boost** — Enhances refinery automation and drone-related production systems for faster incremental progress.

### 🌍 World

- **Unlock Areas** — Provides access to progression-based countryside areas without requiring the usual resource or upgrade milestones.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Combat Mode** — Sludgineers does not feature conventional combat; the trainer focuses on cleanup, mining, refining, upgrades, and exploration.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Load a save and enter an explorable countryside area before activating movement-related options. Use the trainer to reduce travel friction while moving between pollution sites, resource locations, refinery objectives, and quest destinations. Disable any movement enhancement before entering menus or changing areas.

### Tools & Inventory

Collect pollution and mined materials normally, then activate the resource option when you need additional supplies for refinery processing, selling, upgrades, or quests. Avoid changing inventory values while a transaction or processing action is already in progress.

### World & Environment

Progress through countryside levels by cleaning pollution, earning cash and resources, upgrading equipment, building and improving the refinery, completing quests, and unlocking new areas. The trainer can shorten these progression loops, while automation and drones help maintain production between active cleanup sessions.

### Combat & Weapons

Sludgineers has no conventional combat system. The primary activities are using cleanup and mining tools, processing recovered materials, completing quests, and improving the refinery. Combat-related trainer options are therefore not applicable.
